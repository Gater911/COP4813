Spirograph Drawing

Enter values for the outer circle radius, inner circle radius, and pen offset. Select Start Spirograph to draw the pattern on the canvas.

x = (R + r) cos(t) - (r + O) cos(((R + r) / r)t)

y = (R + r) sin(t) - (r + O) sin(((R + r) / r)t)

R is the radius of the outer fixed circle, r is the radius of the rotating inner circle, and O is the pen offset from the center of the inner circle.
