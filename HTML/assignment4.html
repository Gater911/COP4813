Damped Oscillation Calculator

This calculator uses JavaScript to evaluate a damped oscillation equation over a range of x-values and then plots the calculated y-values using Chart.js.

Equation: y = A e-bx cos(ωx)

A is the starting amplitude, b controls how quickly the oscillation decreases, ω controls the oscillation frequency, and x is the independent variable. The calculated value y is plotted on the vertical axis while x is plotted on the horizontal axis.
