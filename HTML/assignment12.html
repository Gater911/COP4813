Comparison Summary

WordPress, Drupal, and Joomla are traditional general-purpose CMS platforms that can render full websites directly. Ghost is more specialized around publishing, newsletters, memberships, and subscriptions. Strapi is different because it is a headless CMS: it manages and delivers content through APIs while a separate front end displays the content.

The best CMS depends on the project. A site that needs a large plugin ecosystem may favor WordPress, while complex structured content and editorial workflows can make Drupal a strong option. Joomla provides a feature-rich traditional CMS with strong multilingual and ACL tools. Ghost fits publishing-focused organizations, and Strapi fits API-first projects where developers want complete control over the front end.
