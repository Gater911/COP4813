Interactive Bouncing Ball Animation

This animation uses JavaScript and the HTML5 canvas. The ball has an animated face, watches the cursor, becomes more frightened as the cursor gets closer, and dynamically rolls away.

Click the ball on the ground to make it jump. Clicking it again while it is in the air changes its color. If it is trapped in a corner it shakes in fear, and a high fall creates a visible impact.

Animation Canvas

Move the cursor near the ball. Click the ball to make it jump.
